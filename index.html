Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

Delivery Most standard interior renders are delivered within 48–72 hours. Complex scenes, animations, or bulk projects may take 4–7 days. We always confirm the timeline before starting work.
File formats We accept 3ds Max, SketchUp, Blender, Revit, AutoCAD, and OBJ/FBX files. If you only have floor plans or reference images, we can work with those too — just share what you have.
Revisions Every project includes up to 2–4 rounds of revisions at no extra cost. This covers changes to camera angles, lighting, materials, and minor layout adjustments. Additional revisions are available at a small fee.
Output Our standard output is 4K (3840×2160px). We also deliver in custom resolutions based on your requirement — print, web, or presentation. Files are exported as JPG, PNG, or EXR.
Modeling Yes. If you only have drawings, sketches, or reference photos, our team can build the 3D model from scratch. Modeling is quoted separately based on project complexity.
Getting started Simply email us at info.rendervertex@gmail.com. Share your files and brief, and we'll send a quote within 24 hours. No commitment needed to get a quote.
